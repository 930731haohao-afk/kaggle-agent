# Archived prose knowledge files — superseded 2026-08-07

These files were the hand-written knowledge library. They are ARCHIVED, not deleted, because
they are the historical record of what the recorded benchmark runs read.

They are no longer read by anything: four adversarial verification rounds showed that
per-competition redaction of prose leaks through channels no scrubber sees (positional
aggregates, headings, paragraph subject drift). The library now lives in
`knowledge/knowledge_base.json` — structured records with competition-free prose and
per-fact competition slugs — and every view is RENDERED per competition by
`knowledge/task_priors_for.py`. Do not read these files during a benchmark run; do not
write back here. Write-back goes into `knowledge_base.json` as structured evidence items.
